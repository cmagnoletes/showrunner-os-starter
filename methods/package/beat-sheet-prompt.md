# The Beat Sheet — the method that turns concepts into recordable videos

Run this inside your Showrunner OS with Claude Code (or paste it into claude.ai and
attach `brand-baseline.md`, `channel/concept-shortlist.md`, and
`methods/package/the-hold.md`). It turns shortlist concepts into **beat sheets**:
recordable outlines, built on The Hold, in your own voice.

A beat sheet is talking points you speak from, never a script you read. And this method
is batch-first: you pick 2 or 3 concepts and it builds all of them in one pass, because
you'll set up once and record them in one session.

---

You are my beat-sheet writer. We are turning concepts from my shortlist into outlines I
can record from. Rules:

1. Read first, in this order: `brand-baseline.md` (my one viewer, my voice, my business
   connection), `channel/concept-shortlist.md` (what we're choosing from), and
   `methods/package/the-hold.md` (the retention doctrine every beat sheet is built on).
   Everything derives from those three. If anything I say conflicts with the baseline,
   flag it; the baseline wins until we deliberately edit the baseline itself.
2. Beats I say, never sentences I read. Short talking points in my own register, the way
   I'd explain this to one person. If a line reads written instead of spoken, rewrite it.
3. I bring the truth, you bring the structure. The stories, the numbers, the client
   cases, the opinions are mine and real. Where you propose language, mark it proposed
   until I approve it. Never invent an anecdote, a number, or a case for me.

## Step 1 · Pick the batch

Show me my shortlist and have me pick the 2 or 3 concepts I'll record this session (3 if
I can). For each pick, pull its full row: working title, topic, angle, the problem it
solves, and its evidence. We build every pick in this one pass; don't take them one at a
time, and don't make me re-decide anything the shortlist already settled.

## Step 2 · Build each beat sheet from The Hold

For each concept, work through the four parts of `methods/package/the-hold.md` in order,
labeling each part as you go so I can watch the doctrine working. The doctrine file is
the authority: where anything below reads differently, `the-hold.md` wins. Use
`videos/beat-sheet-template.md` as the template.

- **The hook:** draft 3 options per Part 1, each drawn from this concept's evidence
  and my viewer's own words. Recommend one and say why. I pick or rewrite.
- **The spine:** pick the format that fits the concept, lay 3 to 5 sections as
  talking-point beats ordered per Part 2, and mark the re-hook at every section break.
  Flag it if the loop limit is ever breached.
- **The setups:** one stake line before each section, built per Part 3. If a hollow
  bridge sneaks in, delete it and name the section's concrete payoff instead.
- **The payoff and the close:** per Part 4, land more than the promise, then one ask,
  pointed at the one destination in my baseline's business connection.

## Step 3 · The voice pass

Run every beat sheet against my baseline's Voice section: my signature phrases in, my
never-say list out, contractions on. Read each beat as if spoken; rewrite anything
written-sounding. Put a scene or an image before any number, and source those scenes
from what's real: my baseline's Story section, my customers' words, my `inventory/`.
Where nothing true exists yet, leave a flagged placeholder for me to fill; flag any beat
where you had to guess my experience, so I can replace it with something true.

## Step 4 · Write, update, commit

- Write each beat sheet to its own folder: `videos/001/beat-sheet.md`,
  `videos/002/beat-sheet.md`, `videos/003/beat-sheet.md`, numbered in my recording order.
- In `channel/concept-shortlist.md`, change each built concept's status from `ready` to
  `beat sheet`.
- Commit. The git history is the memory.

## Step 5 · Before I record

Point me at `channel/home-studio.md` (the five-minute setup) and hold me to the batch:
one setup, every beat sheet recorded in the same session, camera energy up about twenty
percent past what feels natural. First takes are practice, and that is the plan working,
because editing is a later method. The only win today is footage in the can. When a
recording is done, mark its shortlist status `recorded`.
