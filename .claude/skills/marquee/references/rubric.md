# The grade — 11 criteria, 55 points, 6 gates

A thumbnail is a decision that fires in under one second in a moving feed. The chain
it has to survive: impression, glance, question formed, title read, click, watch,
satisfaction. Every criterion below tests one link. Apply in the order given in the
skill: glance first, then score, then the gates, then the red flags.

## 1 · The one-second glance (precondition)

Look at the image for one second at phone size. Say out loud what the video is about.
If you can't, stop and revise before scoring anything.

## 2 · The eleven criteria (score 1 to 5 each)

| # | Criterion | The question | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clarity / glance | Can a stranger name the subject in one second without the title? | High |
| 2 | Focal-element count | Three elements or fewer competing for attention (face, object, words)? | High |
| 3 | Contrast / salience | Does one element arrest the eye? Is the subject separated from the background? | High |
| 4 | Curiosity gap | Does it withhold enough to force a click? Does it raise a question without answering it? | High |
| 5 | Title-thumbnail split | Do the title and the thumbnail carry different, complementary information? | High |
| 6 | Emotion authenticity | Is the expression genuine, legible, and matched to the video's real tone? | Medium |
| 7 | Mobile legibility | At 168 and 120 pixels wide, do the words read and the subject hold? Are the words out of the right third, and are the words and the face out of the bottom-right corner where YouTube's duration badge sits? (Clothing or background touching that corner is not a miss.) | Medium |
| 8 | Watch-time honesty | Does the video deliver what the thumbnail implies, early? | Medium |
| 9 | Brand consistency | Would this be recognized as the channel's without the name (font, colors, treatment)? | Medium |
| 10 | Authenticity / realism | Does it read as real, not over-polished, not AI-typical? | Lower |
| 11 | Text economy | If words are present: two to five, adding something the title and image don't, or none at all? | Lower |

Scoring guides:

- **Clarity:** 5 the subject is unmistakable; 1 the viewer has to decode it.
- **Elements:** 5 three or fewer; 3 four with a clear hierarchy; 1 five or more.
- **Contrast:** 5 it pops next to competitors in a feed; 1 it blends in.
- **Curiosity:** 5 the viewer must click to resolve it; 1 the image explains the video.
- **Split:** 5 genuinely different information; 1 redundant.
- **Emotion:** 5 genuine and matched; 3 genuine but slightly off-tone; 1 performed or false.
- **Mobile:** 5 everything reads small and sits in safe zones; 1 words or subject vanish.
- **Honesty:** 5 accurate; 1 implies content the video doesn't contain.
- **Brand:** 5 identifiable as the channel; 1 different fonts, colors, style from the rest.
- **Realism:** 5 real and intentional; 3 stylized but plausible; 1 identifiably AI or over-processed.
- **Text:** 5 absent and unneeded, or two to five words that add; 1 repeats the title, exceeds five, or is illegible.

**Thresholds:** 50 to 55 ships as is. 42 to 49 ships after one targeted fix. 33 to 41
needs redesign on two or three criteria. Below 33 goes back to the archetype or the
words. One rule sits above the numbers: a failed gate or a red flag always means at
least one fix, whatever the score.

## 3 · The six pass or fail gates

A failure on any one gate sends the thumbnail back regardless of score.

1. **Glance:** at phone size for one second, the subject is nameable.
2. **Postage stamp:** shrunk to about 120 pixels wide, the subject still reads, the
   words are still legible, the main contrast still holds.
3. **Squint:** blur your eyes; the most important element is still the clearest shape.
4. **Three elements:** count faces, text blocks, objects, overlays; three or fewer.
5. **Title split:** cover the title, write the thumbnail's question; read the title; it
   answers a different question.
6. **Clickbait:** the video delivers what the thumbnail implies within the first
   minute.

## 4 · Red flags (each one is a revision trigger)

- Title words repeated on the thumbnail
- More than three active elements
- A generated, altered, or borrowed face (not the channel owner's own, unaltered photo)
- White text on yellow, or any low-contrast text
- Words in the bottom-left (timestamp) or bottom-right (duration badge) corners, or
  anything critical in the right third (mobile UI)
- A channel logo eating real estate
- The thumbnail answers its own question
- Over-polished, too-symmetric, uncanny
- Exaggerated shock face on an adult-audience channel
- Emotion that mismatches the video's tone
- More than five words
- "Viral" anywhere in the text
- App watermarks
- Lighting on the face that doesn't match the background
- Not 16 by 9, or under 1280 by 720

## 5 · The verdict, in the owner's language

Give the total, the failed gates if any, the red flags if any, and then one sentence:
ships, or the one fix that lifts it most, and why. Never a list of ten small notes; one
fix, rescore, repeat.
